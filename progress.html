# Round 3 builder note: final approach to street-level targets

## Causes found
I traced the critic's autopilot sample by sample. I also read the colliders around each slow target.

1. **The camera never looked down at the pad.** The chase camera keeps a shallow pitch. Take a pad 20 m below and 10 m ahead. It sits about 60 degrees below the horizon. The vertical FOV is only 60 degrees, so the pad leaves the frame. Only a special case for "far and high" existed.
2. **Parapets pinned the drone on roofs.** Every flat roof has a 0.7 m parapet that is solid. A drone that lands on a roof and flies toward the edge hits the parapet with its contact normal mostly horizontal. It bounces back. Holding descend pulls it back down, so it stays inside the parapet ring. This was the main roof stall (seed 44 pharmacy: the pad is 1.6 m past the parapet of a 6.6 m building).
3. **The drone arrived over the pad while still high.** It drops at 10 m/s, and the bot starts its descent 25 m out. It then reaches the pad at 20 to 30 m altitude, hovers, drifts and wobbles, and often settles on the next roof. Boost speed bled off at 35% of the normal drag, so arrivals after a boost overshot by 10 to 20 m.
4. **Tree colliders started too low.** Each one is a box that starts at the lowest point of the canopy. The round canopy is an ellipsoid, so the lower corners of the box were invisible. At street level they wedged the drone between a facade and a tree (seed 22 Records: 4 stalls).
5. **Some pads were hidden behind a tower.** From the arrival side, a tower stood in the way. One example is seed 44 Deli on Copper & 4th, beside a 70 m tower. Coming from the far side, a straight approach runs into the tower wall. This is the critic's original seed-44 screenshot.
6. The trigger radius and the landing tolerance were not the problem. I did not change them.

## Changes (and why)
- **Approach camera** (`CameraRig.ts`, `CAMERA.approach*`). The effect starts when the target is within 45 m and more than 2 m below. It is fully on at 18 m. The camera rises up to 4.5 m and pulls in 18%. It pitches down until the pad sits in the lower part of the frame. The pitch is capped so the drone stays in frame. Only the pitch changes, so the heading still follows the drone. The effect blends in over about 0.4 s.
- **Through-roof ground ring** (`ObjectiveMarker.ts`). This is an additive ring with no depth test, drawn at the pad. You can see where the pad is through roofs and towers from 6 to 90 m. It uses a basic material and no `pow()`. The round-2 beam and wall clamps are untouched.
- **Ledge hop** (`Drone.ts`, `FLIGHT.hop*`). Some obstacles are low: their top is less than 2.2 m above the skids, with nothing taller behind. If you fly into one, the drone skims up over it instead of bouncing. This covers parapets, roof units and fountain rims. Collision still applies. The hop only adds lift.
- **Faster drop, crisper stop** (`FLIGHT.descendSpeed` 16, `sinkBrakeResponse` 7). Climb stays at 10 m/s. Getting down to a street drop now feels snappy. When you release descend, the drop stops fast, so you do not sink past a roof edge.
- **Dive airbrake.** Within 28 m of the pad, when you descend, leftover boost speed bleeds at the normal rate. So you do not sail past the pad.
- **Drop guide** (`FLIGHT.guide*`). The guide works within 11 m of the pad. You must be descending, or hovering less than 6 m above the pad. The drone then eases toward the pad centre at up to 6 m/s. Pulling back weakens the pull, and climbing or boosting cancels it.
- **Tree colliders fit the canopy** (`CityGenerator.ts`). The canopy box now starts where the ellipsoid is as wide as the box (2.2 x scale instead of 1.6 x scale). A thin trunk collider was added. Pines are unchanged.
- **Pad placement** (`DeliverySystem.ts`). When possible, the game picks a next target that you can glide to. The test line runs from 25 m out on the arrival side to the pad, and must clear solid buildings. This uses the same `isClear` test the first pickup already used. When nothing else fits, hidden pads still appear.

# Round 2 builder note: blank desktop render with bloom on

## Root cause
The objective sky beam (and its ring wall) shader computed `pow(1.0 - vUv.y, ...)`. The composer renders into a 4x MSAA HalfFloat target, and on partly covered edge pixels MSAA evaluates varyings at the pixel centre, so `vUv.y` lands just above 1.0 on the beam's top rim and `pow()` of a negative base returns NaN (about 15 to 18 pixels near the top of the screen). The clamp pass lets NaN through (NaN fails every comparison), and UnrealBloomPass blurs it across every mip, so the composite becomes NaN everywhere and the output shows one flat colour.

How it was proven: per-pass readback on a blank frame showed NaN first appearing in the RenderPass output (18 values), the high-pass target at 9 values, and the bloom output at 100% NaN. Hiding each visible object in turn on that frame showed that only hiding the beam mesh brought the NaN count to 0. The same frame rendered to non-MSAA Half or Float targets had no NaN, which matches the MSAA extrapolation explanation.

## Fix
`src/entities/ObjectiveMarker.ts`: clamp `vUv.y` (wall and beam) and `vFacing` (beam) to [0,1] before `pow()`. No change to the post chain, bloom settings or quality. The look is unchanged.

# Drone Dash, round 1 critic report

**Verdict:** A charming, well-built arcade courier game that is unplayable on desktop as shipped. With bloom on (the desktop default), the 3D view turns into a flat orange screen for most of the flight.

## The single biggest gap: the desktop render goes blank in flight

With the default desktop settings (post-processing on), the whole 3D view regularly turns into one flat peach-orange colour. Only the HUD stays visible. This happens while you climb, boost, hover high, and even hover still at 14 m. It lasts for seconds at a time, and some single frames flicker back to normal, so it strobes. With `?post=0` the same flight renders perfectly. On touch devices post-processing is off by default, so mobile does not have the bug.

Evidence:
- I read back pixels inside the page after each frame (9 sample points per frame, Apple M5 Metal GPU through ANGLE). While I held W+Space for 3 s, **187 of 331 frames (56%)** were one flat colour with post on. With `?post=1` it was 202/348, and with `?post=0` it was **0/361**. Script: `critic-scripts/pixel-probe.mjs`, `pixel-probe2.mjs`.
- Key probe (`critic-scripts/key-probe.mjs`): standing still and W alone render fine. After about 0.6 s of W+Space the view goes flat. W+E (boost) was flat for 6 of 8 screenshots.
- In a full autopilot run, my screenshot helper retried 25 times over about 1 s and still could not get a non-blank frame at four key moments: `critic/d03-flying-BLANK.png`, `d04-near-pickup-BLANK.png`, `d05-parcel-picked-BLANK.png`, `d08b-after-bump-BLANK.png`. Also `d10-blank-frame-post-on.png` and `probe-boost-held.png`.
- The same moments with post off look great: `critic/nopost-d03-flying.png`, `nopost-d08b-after-bump.png`, `nopost-d06-carrying-enroute.png`.
- The camera is not inside a building. During blank frames it was in open air at y 40 to 48 (logged in the probe run). The background colour you see is the sky/fog horizon colour, which means the post chain outputs a flat result. A NaN or Inf from an HDR highlight that spreads through bloom is a likely cause, but the builder must find the real cause.

## Other gaps for later rounds (ranked)
1. **Arrival is fiddly.** Pickup radius is 3.2 m and the vertical tolerance is tight. My autopilot circled a rooftop pickup 4.4 m away for over 4 s, and twice hovered on a roof edge 21 to 24 m from a street-level drop it could not drop down to (`nopost-d08-stuck-near-drop.png`). A good arcade courier game pulls you into the zone at the end of the approach, or shows a clear landing cone so the player knows where to descend.
2. **Collisions feel like walls of rubber, not crashes.** I logged 5 to 7 bumps per run. Speed drops from about 30 to 10 m/s with a small flash. There is no visible drone wobble or spin and no lasting penalty, so walls never feel dangerous. The timer is the only stake.
3. **Boost is tiny on mobile.** One 2 s boost press drained the bar almost to empty and flung the drone out over the water (`m03-boost-turn.png`). The boost button sits right next to climb, so it is easy to hit by mistake.
4. **Run start is disorienting.** At 0.3 s the title text is still fading over the gameplay view, and the target is 78 m away behind your left shoulder (`d02-takeoff.png`). The drone should spawn facing the first pickup with the camera already behind it.
5. **No sense of difficulty curve.** Three legs per run, all of the same kind. Time bonuses (+19 s) make the clock generous early and nothing ramps up. Two deliveries scored 1,188 and earned "New best" (`d09-game-over.png`).

What already meets the bar: the golden-hour art direction, readable building silhouettes, the drone model with banking, the strong objective beam and pad ring (`nopost-d08b-after-bump.png`), the edge-of-screen nav arrow with distance, the clear pop text and combo chip (`d07-delivery.png`), and a clean game-over card. The best polished browser arcade flyers have a camera that never hides the target, a world that reads at a glance, landings that snap in, and impacts you feel in the controller. The main things missing here are the stable render and the landing snap.

## Performance
- Desktop, 1280x720, Apple M5 on a 120 Hz display, about 85 s of active autopilot flight (10,283 rAF samples): **median 8.3 ms, p95 9.1 ms, p99 9.3 ms, max 10.3 ms, 0 frames over 20 ms.** In an earlier run there was a single 300 ms hitch on the first gameplay frame, likely shader compile. It is locked at 120 fps, so 60 fps is met with large headroom. The blank frames cost no time, so they are not a perf symptom.
- With post off: median 8.3 ms, p95 9.0 ms, one 151 ms hitch at start.
- Draw calls 71 to 85, about 291k triangles.
- First-load transfer (dev server, unminified): **6.95 MB over 35 requests**, under the 15 MB budget. The existing `dist/` is 4.1 MB on disk.
- Mobile (iPhone 13 landscape viewport, DPR 2, post off): 120 fps reported most of the time. One reading of 33.7 fps came during a boost and turn with two CDP touches held. This is unconfirmed and needs a real device check.
