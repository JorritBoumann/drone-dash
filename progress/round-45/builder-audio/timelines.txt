Drone Dash, round 45 BUILDER audio clips: what happens when (same ten clips, seeds and routes as the audio critic)
==================================================================================================================

How the clips were made
- Real master output of the page (after the game's glue compressor, limiter and trim), stereo, 48 kHz, recorded in real
  time from headless Chromium at 1280x720 with music on and sound effects on. Nothing was re-synthesised.
- Recorder: builder-scripts/rec45b.mjs, a copy of the critic's rec45.mjs with the same pilot, seed 22, routes, test
  hooks and clip plan. Only the tap differs: the game now exposes its mix tap points (audioTaps), so the capture has
  seven channels: master L/R, music bus, effects bus (dry), drone bus (rotor, air, slipstream, boost burner; after its
  duck), ambience bus, effects return (reverb, slapback, ping-pong). Own dev server on 5197 (not the owner's 5188).
- Cutting and measuring: builder-scripts/analyze45b.py (the critic's analyze45.py plus the ambience channel and extra
  bands). Numbers: audio-measurements.json in this folder.
- The scripted pilot flies in real time, so the run differs a little from the critic's (score, number of drops, where
  the payouts fall). Clips 03, 04 and 06 are picked by the same rules (best chain window, longest boost, the pickup
  before the first medal).
- Levels in brackets: "cue vs music" = the cue bus against the ducked music bus in the cue's window (window RMS, so
  very short cues such as ticks read low here; builder-scripts/cuepeak45b.log has the loudest-20-ms numbers);
  "duck" = how far the music bus actually dropped; "cue vs rotor+wind" = against the drone bus.

What each event sounds like now (src/systems/AudioSystem.ts): see progress/round-45/builder.md, "What changed per sound".
