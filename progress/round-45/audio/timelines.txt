Drone Dash, round 45 audio clips: what happens when, and which sounds exist in the code
=======================================================================================

How the clips were made
- Real master output of the page (after the game's glue compressor, limiter and trim), stereo, 48 kHz, recorded in
  real time from headless Chromium at 1280x720 with music on and sound effects on. Nothing was re-synthesised.
- Pilot: a scripted routed street pilot (the round 5 to 11 low line): corners at 7 m/s, 16 m/s on straights, 7 m up,
  boost held on long straight streets. Seed 22. First run in a fresh browser, so the game's teaching mode is on.
- Sessions: "main" = title, a full run (score 9,116, Gold, 5 drops, 0 crashes) and the game-over screen.
  "fx" = a scripted scrape and head-on crash, then the drone is moved by a test hook to the harbour (follows the
  harbour bus 3.5 m over the water) and then to the busiest street (2.8 m up, past people and cars).
  "ot" = the same pilot with the start clock shortened to 22 s (test hook), so the clock runs out mid-delivery and
  overtime starts. Clips with two segments are joined with a 0.3 s crossfade; the join time is given.
- Levels in brackets: "cue vs music" = the cue bus against the ducked music bus in the cue's window; "duck" = how far
  the music bus actually dropped; "cue vs rotor+wind" = cue against the rotor, wind and slipstream air bed.

Sounds in the code for each event (src/systems/AudioSystem.ts)
- take-off: start() = four rising triangle notes G C E G. Rotor on: setRotor(true).
- always in flight: rotor = two detuned sawtooth oscillators (88 to 214 Hz) into a resonant lowpass (600 to 2,800 Hz),
  amplitude-modulated at 0.42 x pitch; wind = looped white noise, bandpass 400 to 1,800 Hz, louder with speed and
  headwind; slipstream air = the same noise through a narrow bandpass (Q 6) that whistles up 700 to 2,500 Hz.
- slipstream meter quarter: slipTick() = one triangle blip, up 3 semitones per step.
- slipstream payout: slipPayout(chain) = noise whoosh + triangle + sine chime + 1 to 4 sine arpeggio notes, a whole
  tone higher per chain step; music ducks to 0.45. Silent before the game introduces the slipstream on a first run.
- pickup: pickup() = three sine notes E G C plus triangle octaves and a high noise tick; music ducks to 0.6.
- delivery: deliver(combo) = five-note sine chord C E G C E (lifted by combo) + low sine + air noise; duck 0.45.
- medal: medal(tier) = square + sine three-note fanfare G C E, held chord, 1 to 4 sine bells, air noise; duck 0.28.
- boost start: boost() = bandpass noise sweep 400 to 2,600 Hz + soft saw glide 220 to 440 Hz. While boosting the
  rotor pitch, filter and gain rise. No sound for boost end (the rotor just glides back).
- scrape: scrape(strength) = two short noise bursts (bandpass 5.2 to 2.6 kHz and highpass 7 kHz), rate-limited.
- knock / light hit: bump(strength) = sine thud 140 to 45 Hz + lowpass noise + tiny square click.
- head-on crash: impact(s) = sine thud 95 to 38 Hz + lowpass noise crunch + bandpass noise clank + rotor sputter
  (rotor gain stepped 7 times in 0.55 s, rotor pitch dropped 9 semitones) + square whine; duck 0.35. Then
  crashCue(comboBroken) = falling two-note triangle "lost it". No sound for the slow-motion beat itself.
- rush hour: rushHour() = two-note square + sine horn, twice, low sine, air noise; duck 0.4.
- rush order: rushOrder() = two square beeps. Rush missed: tick().
- last 10 s: tick(urgent) = one 70 ms square beep, 1,175 Hz (1,480 Hz in the last 5 s). Music switches to "final".
- overtime: overtimeHorn() = rising three-note square + sine horn and a noise snare roll; duck 0.4.
- game over: gameOver(tier) = falling triangle sting (no medal) or rising triangle cadence, sine chord and bells.
- menus: click() = one 60 ms triangle at 880 Hz (title buttons, play, resume). Nothing for pause, music toggle,
  minimap toggle or the game-over buttons beyond that click.
- no sound at all for: pedestrians (or their reactions), cars and vans, the harbour bus, boats, water, gulls, city
  ambience, time bonus on delivery, combo up, slow clock, teaching pop-ups, pad landing, minimap.
